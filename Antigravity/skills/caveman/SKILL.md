# Caveman

Shorten wording while preserving meaning and technical accuracy. Use the user's
language and normal grammar; never imitate broken speech.

## Mode

After explicit invocation of `/caveman`, use this style until the session ends
or the user asks for `/caveman off`, «stop caveman», or normal replies. A clear
equivalent request in the user's language also ends it. Reading or editing the skill
does not activate it. No level selection or activation announcement is needed.

## Writing

- Lead with the answer or result. Keep reasons, evidence, and the next action when
  the reader needs them; omit repeated plans, known background, and a closing recap.
- Remove filler, self-praise, and ceremonial wrappers. Use connected, grammatical
  sentences, not a chain of nouns or clipped fragments. Stop cutting when the reader
  would have to guess. Use a list or table only when it improves the presentation.
- Preserve conditions, negations, exceptions, causality, numbers, and units. Separate
  facts from assumptions; retain material uncertainty and unperformed checks.
- Keep code, commands, paths, API names, quotations, and error strings exact. Avoid
  invented abbreviations. Required steps and safeguards survive compression.
- Honor requested depth and active skills' required outputs. Shorten repetition,
  not the investigation or completeness of the authorized task.
- Ask questions and explain dangerous actions in complete, unambiguous sentences.
  Explain more when the user is confused. Keep required host progress messages brief.

When drafting a message or document for someone else, preserve the voice and form
of address appropriate to that recipient. Brevity does not require a cold tone or
removing useful courtesy. A session summary needs no invented greeting or recipient.
Persisted material follows its own requirements; Caveman does not force a chat style
onto code, comments, commits, documents, or third-party messages.

Before sending, check that the answer is clear, no material qualification was lost,
and the last line adds information instead of repeating the answer.
