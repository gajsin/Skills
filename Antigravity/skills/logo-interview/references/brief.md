# Current brief and handover

Maintain one concise current brief in chat. Use a file only when persistence or a destination is authorized; do not archive the full transcript or create a module hierarchy by default.

## Useful contents

Include only what this project needs:

- Task/scope, exact name/script, new identity or refresh.
- Purpose, audience/context, applications, and essential constraints.
- Character in the user's words; associations and whether they are metaphorical or literal.
- Concept: what the mark stands for, its basis, and any intended recognizable element.
- Visual direction: shown board/reference links, useful traits, and reasons.
- Candidates: stable IDs, source files, revisions, and selection status.
- Decisions at each level below: confirmed, tentative, rejected with reason, open, or delegated.
- Remaining freedom, meaningful uncertainty, and the next requested action.

Another designer should be able to continue without repeating the interview. Keep facts distinguishable from interpretations and reception hypotheses.

## Decision levels

| Level | What can be settled |
|---|---|
| Character | Intended qualities and associations |
| Visual territory | Form language, reference principles, relevant type/color relationships |
| Concept | What identifies the owner and the idea behind the mark |
| Construction | A particular candidate's silhouette and arrangement |
| Details | Weight, spacing, curve tension, optical adjustments |

Record approval only at the supported level. A preferred property leaves other constructions open; a chosen construction focuses work on its remaining details. Preserve the user's reasons and explicitly delegated freedom. Keep a board's hypothesis separate from confirmed taste.

## Confirm and revise

Summarize consequential conclusions and invite specific corrections. Respect approval already given; do not repeat it as a ritual.

When preferences change, update the same brief and affected visuals, preserve unrelated decisions and prior assets, and confirm only the consequential new choice. Label superseded work. For authorized file revisions, update the current brief or create a sibling version without overwriting other assets.

On resume, recover the latest record and continue from the requested revision or material gap. If it is unavailable, ask for the summary rather than pretending to remember.

## Completion

State what was viewed/generated, agreed, inferred, and untested. Without relevant viewed visuals, the result is a provisional text brief. An agreed board does not establish a final logo, exact fonts, production readiness, audience reception, originality, or trademark clearance. End with the next useful action within the requested scope.
