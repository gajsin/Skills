## Additional compositions

- **W01 — Waterline.** One boundary crosses the whale and changes the silhouette
  from dark above the water to light below it.
- **P01 — Press.** A cropped mechanism drives the eye into the inscription on
  the curved paper.
- **P02 — Trail.** A sparse field and a trail of receipts lead to a visible
  boundary.
- **P03 — Overprint.** Torn strips and displaced type form the main image.
